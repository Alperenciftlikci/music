Delete Your Data

Applies to Music AI by Alperen Çiftlikçi on Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store).

Music AI does not use accounts, logins or passwords. The first time you open the app, it creates an anonymous identifier on your device, and everything you make is stored under that identifier. You can delete all of it at any time.

Delete from inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open Music AI.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Delete All Data and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Please delete before uninstalling. Uninstalling the app removes the anonymous identifier from your device, but it does not delete the data on our servers.

What is deleted

  • Songs you generated, including their titles, lyrics, prompts and audio links
  • Playlists and favorites
  • Your generation history and any queued or in-progress generation requests
  • Usage counters and rate-limit records
  • Your anonymous account record (credits and plan status)

What is kept

  • Content reports you submitted using "Report Content". These contain your anonymous identifier, the reported song and the text you wrote. They are kept for moderation, safety and legal compliance.
  • Purchase and subscription records held by Google Play, Apple and our subscription processor (RevenueCat), which are required for billing, tax and refunds.

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription

Android: open the Google Play Store → your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Music AI → Cancel.
iOS: open Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Music AI → Cancel.

Can't open the app anymore?

Email aimusicgenerator.app@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". If you had a subscription, include your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) or your App Store purchase date so we can locate your records. We process requests within 7 days.
